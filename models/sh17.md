## Classes (from the weights file — differs from the SH17 README order)

`0 person · 1 ear · 2 ear-mufs · 3 face · 4 face-guard · 5 face-mask · 6 foot · 7 tool · 8 glasses · 9 gloves · 10 helmet · 11 hands · 12 head · 13 medical-suit · 14 shoes · 15 safety-suit · 16 safety-vest`

**Derived classes** (produced by `vision/ppe.py`, `source="derived"`, `cls_id=null`): `no-safety-glasses` (eye band of a face), `no-helmet` (top of a head), `no-gloves` (bare hand), `no-safety-vest` (torso band of a person; SH17 `safety-vest` only — hi-vis jackets aren't a class).

## Published results (SH17 authors, validation set)

YOLOv8 mAP50 58.0 (n) → 69.3 (x). **No per-class AP is published**, so glasses accuracy is unknown until measured on our data.

## Known limitations

- `glasses` = **any eyewear**: sunglasses and prescription glasses count as compliant. Distinguishing safety glasses needs our own labelled data.
- Trained on mostly eye-level photos; our camera looks **up** from ~20 cm (≈25° at 3 m). Face/glasses accuracy from below is unmeasured.
- Faces below `ppe.rules.*.min_subject_px` (24 px) get no verdict — at 3 m+ that depends on the resolution the phone sends.
